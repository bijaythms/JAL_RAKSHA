Jal Raksha — from data to decision, from risk to resilience
Real-World Issue: Catastrophic flash floods from sudden dam breach & natural lake blockages (Rishi Ganga 2021, Phuktal 2015, South Lhonak 2023, Kosi 2008), lacking automated rapid HADR simulation.

Why Important:

  • 🌊India houses 5,745+ large dams; hundreds exceed 50-year design lifespans near dense river valleys.
  • ⏱️Steep valley flash floods arrive in under 2 to 30 mins — every lost second costs lives.
  • 🏔️Glacial Lake Outbursts (GLOF) & landslide blockages strike Himalayan valleys with zero warning.
  • 📉Conventional tools (HEC-RAS, MIKE FLOOD) take days; commanders need sub-minute mapping.
Solution: Jal Raksha integrates Copernicus GLO-30 DEM, satellite radar (Sentinel-1 SAR), high-density river networks for 65+ Indian dams, dual hydrodynamic solvers (SPH + Delft3D SWE), and automated GEE ground-truth validation. Generates instant Shapefiles (.shp), Google Earth KMLs, and tactical evacuation plans in 15 seconds.

TECHNICAL APPROACH & IMPLEMENTATION

1. Terrain Ingestion & D8 Routing

Copernicus GLO-30 DEM, D8 downhill flow direction, 65+ dam river network centerlines (150-400 nodes).

2. Breach Mechanics & Kinematics

Froehlich (2008), MacDonald & Langridge, geotechnical erosion (k_d, tau_c) producing peak hydrograph Q(t).

3. SPH & Delft3D Hydrodynamic Routing

Lagrangian particle shockwave tracking + 2D DEM bathtub cross-section solver with 1D Muskingum-Cunge diffusion fallback.

4. HADR Decision Support & GIS Export

Time-stepped arrival contours, building/population exposure triage, instant .shp & .kml generation, GEE SAR cross-check.
